Privacy Policy

Xuong Video · Last updated: 16 November 2026

Xuong Video is a personal desktop app used by one creator to produce videos for their own YouTube channel. The app runs on the creator's own computers. There is no Xuong Video server: the developer does not receive, store or see any user data.

Google data the app accesses

  • Google account email (openid, userinfo.email): shown in the app's settings so the creator knows which account is signed in.
  • Google Drive — files created by the app (drive.file): the app saves backups of its own workspace (scripts, settings, video projects) to a folder it creates. It cannot see any other file in the user's Drive.
  • Google Drive — app data folder (drive.appdata): a hidden folder used to keep the app's settings in sync between the creator's two computers.
  • YouTube (youtube, youtube.readonly, youtube.force-ssl): upload videos to the creator's own channel, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publishing time, read the channel's videos and playlists, read public comments on videos, and post one comment from the channel on its own videos.
  • YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly): read the channel's own statistics (views, watch time, audience retention) to show how videos perform.

How the data is used

Google data is used only to provide the features above to the creator. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone else.

To suggest video topics and plans, the app may send channel statistics, video titles and short excerpts of public comments to the Anthropic Claude API, using the creator's own Anthropic account. This is done only to produce suggestions shown to the creator. Google user data is not used to train AI models.

Xuong Video's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data is stored

Sign-in tokens and app data are stored on the creator's own computers (readable only by the computer's owner) and in the creator's own Google Drive. Nothing is stored on servers run by the developer.

Deleting data and revoking access

  • Sign out in the app (Settings → Google → Sign out); this also revokes the app's tokens.
  • Remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the "Xưởng video" folder in Google Drive and the app's data folder on the computer to remove all stored data.

YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Tóm tắt tiếng Việt

Xuong Video chạy trên máy của chủ kênh, không có máy chủ riêng. App chỉ thấy file nó tự tạo trên Google Drive, dùng quyền YouTube để đăng video và đọc số liệu của chính kênh đó. Dữ liệu không bán, không chia sẻ; số liệu kênh và đoạn bình luận có thể gửi tới Claude (Anthropic) bằng tài khoản của chủ kênh để gợi ý chủ đề. Thu hồi quyền ở myaccount.google.com/permissions.
